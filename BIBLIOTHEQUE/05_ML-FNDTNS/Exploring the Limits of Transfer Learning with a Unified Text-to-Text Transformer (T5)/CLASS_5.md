# Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (2020)
Source: Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (T5).md

## Core reasons
- Introduces a unified text-to-text transfer learning framework for NLP tasks, focusing on training methodology rather than positional encoding changes or higher-dimensional adaptations.
- Presents a systematic empirical study comparing pre-training objectives, architectures, datasets, and transfer approaches, aligning with ML methodology and scaling foundations.

## Evidence extracts
- "In this paper, we explore the landscape of transfer learning techniques for NLP by introducing a unified framework that converts all text-based language problems into a text-to-text format." (Abstract)
- "Our systematic study compares pre-training objectives, architectures, unlabeled data sets, transfer approaches, and other factors on dozens of language understanding tasks." (Abstract)

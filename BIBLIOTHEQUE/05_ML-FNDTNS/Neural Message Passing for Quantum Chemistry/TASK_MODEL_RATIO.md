1. **Number of distinct tasks evaluated: 13**

   "QM9 consists of 130k molecules with 13 properties for each molecule which are approximated by an expensive quantum mechanical simulation method (DFT), to yield 13 corresponding regression tasks." (Section 1. Introduction)

2. **Number of trained model instances required to cover all tasks: 1 model**

   "Note, unless otherwise indicated, all tables display result of models trained individually on each target (as opposed to training one model to predict all 13)." (Section 8. Results)

   "We also found that training one model per target consistently outperformed jointly training on all 13 targets." (Section 8. Results)

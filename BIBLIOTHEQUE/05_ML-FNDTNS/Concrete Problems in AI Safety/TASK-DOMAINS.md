## Summary
The paper is a survey of accident risks in machine learning systems and presents five safety research problems rather than defining specific task formulations. It frames the discussion at the level of broad paradigms like supervised learning systems and reinforcement learning agents, without specifying concrete task inputs or outputs. Consequently, the task dimensions, dynamics, attention dynamics, and state dynamics are not specified in the paper.

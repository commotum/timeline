## Summary
The paper covers three core task domains around large language models: text generation, synthetic-text detection, and bias probing/measurement. Inputs are consistently token sequences, while outputs are either generated token sequences (generation) or categorical judgments/probabilities (detection and bias analysis). Most Dimension/Dynamics/Attention/State labels are inferred from the described token-processing pipelines, finite sampling/evaluation setups, and explicit category-based analysis procedures. The paper also mentions other downstream applications, but does not provide equivalent task-interface detail for them here.

## Evidence
### Task: Text generation
- "GPT-2 is a large-scale unsupervised language model that generates coherent paragraphs of text, first announced by OpenAI in February 2019 [65]." (Section Overview)
- "- Generating text from the model \"out of the box\" (e.g. zero-shot generation);" (Section 4 Social Impacts of Large Language Models)
- "- Generating specific styles of text after the model has been trained further (fine-tuned) on a different dataset;" (Section 4 Social Impacts of Large Language Models)
- Inference: `1D (t)`, `Capped`, `Static`, and `Direct` are inferred because the task is described as token-sequence prompting/continuation with finite generated samples rather than open-ended interactive control. Supporting text includes the generation setup: "We generated approximately 50 outputs from each model..." (Appendix D, Section 1 Methodology).

### Task: Synthetic text detection (real vs fake classification)
- "- Discriminating between synthetic text generated by a language model (especially adversarial examples) and human-authored text;" (Section 4 Social Impacts of Large Language Models)
- "- 1. Simple classifiers: Uses classifiers trained from scratch to discriminate between outputs from a language model and some base \"true\" distribution." (Section 4.3 Automated ML-based detection)
- "Our classifier is able to detect 1.5 billion parameter GPT-2-generated text with approximately 95% accuracy." (Section 4.3 Our Work: 1.5 Billion Parameter Model Detection Research)
- Inference: `1D (t)` input and `0D` output are inferred from sequence-classifier framing over text; `Capped` is inferred from explicit fixed-length evaluation ("testing 510-token test examples"); `Static` and `Direct` are inferred because the detector classifies provided text directly without described runtime retrieval/planning (Section 4.3).

### Task: Bias probing and bias attribute classification
- "- Analyzing model activations and outputs scientifically to understand its knowledge and biases." (Section 4 Social Impacts of Large Language Models)
- "A bias probe is an input to a model designed to elucidate the model's disposition towards producing certain kinds of outputs." (Section 4.4 Bias: Exploratory Research, footnote 21)
- "We calculated the probability of an attribute giving an input by reviewing a minimum of 100 outputs... then reviewed outputs to determine the category of attribute (e.g. male, female, or genderless for gender bias)." (Appendix C: Examples of Biases in GPT-2)
- Inference: `1D (t)` input and `0D` output are inferred from prompt-to-category analysis; `Capped` is inferred from finite prompt/output sampling; `Dynamic` attention and `Constructed` state are inferred because the procedure aggregates many outputs and constructs derived bias categories/probabilities rather than using a single direct label mapping.

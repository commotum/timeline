## Summary
The paper covers byte-level language modeling/generation and several text understanding tasks: sentiment analysis, subjectivity/objectivity detection, opinion polarity classification, paraphrase detection, and semantic relatedness. Inputs are textual byte sequences (1D (t)), while outputs include generated byte sequences (1D (t)) and single labels or scores (0D). The model uses hidden-state representations as constructed state, but input/output dynamics and attention behavior are not explicitly specified.

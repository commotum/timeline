## Summary
Gato is presented as a generalist model covering text dialogue, image captioning, visual question answering, and control/manipulation tasks in simulated and real environments. The inputs span text tokens, images, and control observations, yielding 1D (t) and 2D (x, y) dimensions; outputs include text and action tokens. Dynamics are mostly unspecified, except where the paper indicates capped control contexts and fixed-size action specifications (and fixed-length RGB stacking episodes); attention and state dynamics are not specified.

### Task: robot manipulation (block stacking)
- "As a testbed for taking physical actions in the real world, we chose the robotic block stacking environment introduced by Lee et al. (2021)." (Section 3.3 Robotics - RGB Stacking Benchmark)
- "The environment consists of a Sawyer robot arm with 3-DoF cartesian velocity control, an additional DoF for velocity, and a discrete gripper action." (Section 3.3 Robotics - RGB Stacking Benchmark)
- "The available observations include two  $128 \times 128$  camera images, robot arm and gripper joint angles as well as the robot's end-effector pose." (Section 3.3 Robotics - RGB Stacking Benchmark)
- "Episodes have a fixed length of 400 timesteps at 20 Hz" (Section 3.3 Robotics - RGB Stacking Benchmark)
- Inference: Input dynamics marked Fixed because episodes have a "fixed length of 400 timesteps" and fixed-size camera images; output dynamics marked Fixed because action specifications are fixed ("3-DoF cartesian velocity control" and a "discrete gripper action"). (Section 3.3 Robotics - RGB Stacking Benchmark)

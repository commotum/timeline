## Summary
Consistency models are presented as noise-to-image generators and evaluated on image datasets. The paper also demonstrates zero-shot image editing tasks including denoising, interpolation, inpainting, colorization, super-resolution, and stroke-guided generation. Dimensions are 2D (x, y) inferred from image framing, while dynamics, attention, and state are not specified in the paper.

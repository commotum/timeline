## Summary
The paper covers one task intent: class-conditional image generation on ImageNet using latent diffusion with a transformer backbone. The modeled objects are spatial image latents and decoded images (2D (x, y)), with timestep/class conditioning introducing temporal and scalar components that map to 1D (t) and 0D labels (inferred). The interface is bounded by explicit resolution, patch-size, and diffusion-step limits, so input/output dynamics are capped (inferred). Attention is static and state is direct (inferred) in the described standard transformer denoising pipeline.

## Evidence
### Task: Class-conditional image generation
- "We train class-conditional latent DiT models at  $256 \times 256$  and  $512 \times 512$  image resolution on the ImageNet dataset [31], a highly-competitive generative modeling benchmark." (Section 4. Experimental Setup)
- "Conditional diffusion models take extra information as input, such as a class label c." (Section 3.1. Preliminaries, Classifier-free guidance)
- "The input to DiT is a spatial representation z (for  $256 \times 256 \times 3$  images, z has shape  $32 \times 32 \times 4$ )." (Section 3.2. Diffusion Transformer Design Space, Patchify)
- "Given patch size  $p \times p$ , a spatial representation (the noised latent from the VAE) of shape  $I \times I \times C$  is \"patchified\" into a sequence of length  $T = (I/p)^2$  with hidden dimension d." (Figure 4)
- "we use a  $t_{\rm max} = 1000$  linear variance schedule ranging from  $1 \times 10^{-4}$  to  $2 \times 10^{-2}$" (Section 4. Experimental Setup, Diffusion)
- "New images can then be generated by sampling a representation z from the diffusion model and subsequently decoding it to an image with the learned decoder x = D(z)." (Section 3.1. Preliminaries, Latent diffusion models)
- "Both of these outputs have shape equal to the original spatial input." (Section 3.2. Diffusion Transformer Design Space, Transformer decoder)
- "We aim to be as faithful to the standard transformer architecture as possible to retain its scaling properties." (Section 3.2. Diffusion Transformer Design Space)
- Inference: In Dimension and Out Dimension are inferred from explicit spatial latent/image shapes and timestep conditioning ("z has shape  $32 \times 32 \times 4$ "; "sequence of length  $T = (I/p)^2$ "; " $t_{\rm max} = 1000$ "). In Dynamics and Out Dynamics are inferred as Capped because the paper uses bounded resolutions (256 and 512), bounded patch sizes (p = 2, 4, 8), and a bounded diffusion horizon. Attention Dynamic is inferred as Static because DiT applies standard transformer self-attention over the provided token sequence. State Dynamic is inferred as Direct because the denoising mapping is described as stepwise prediction from current noised latent and conditions, without a persistent external memory/state mechanism.

## 11. Architectural Workarounds

- Two-stage processing (surrounding-view space and ray-cast space) to integrate source-view relationships and ray-local consistency ("The learning process of TransNeRF is divided into two stages: the first deals with the surrounding-view space and the second deals with in the ray-cast space, shown in Fig 3." (I. INTRODUCTION)).
- Local ray attention over neighboring points to model local consistency along the ray ("our TransNeRF considers adjacent 3D points on the target ray simultaneously by attention layers in Density-RayDecoder and Color-RayDecoder." (I. INTRODUCTION); "For the query point  $q^i$  and neighboring 2n points  $\{q^{i-n},\cdots,q^{i-1},q^{i+1},\cdots,q^{i+n}\}$  along the target-viewing ray, we denote  $[\sigma_0^{i-n};\cdots;\sigma_0^{i-1};\sigma_0^{i};\sigma_0^{i+1};\cdots;\sigma_0^{i+n}]$  as their initial density representations at the input end of the Density-RayDecoder." (III.B)).
- Positional encodings added to ray-cast sequences to preserve position information ("positional encodings  $\mathbf{E}^{pos}$  are added to density representations of neighboring 3D points to keep their position information in the ray-cast space." (III.B)).
- Pre-trained U-Net feature extractor for source-view feature volumes ("For each observed source view, we first extract its feature volume from a pre-trained U-Net and retrieve spatial-aligned 2D-pixel features for the query 3D point as its initial source-view feature." (III. METHODOLOGY)).
- Permutation-invariant attention to accept arbitrary numbers of source views ("Our Density-ViewDecoder is invariant to permutations of source views and can receive an arbitrary number of source views." (III.A)).

### 13. Constraint Profile (Synthesis)

**Constraint Profile:**
- Domain scope: Multi-view RGB images across real and synthetic datasets; same modality, multiple domains.
- Task structure: Single task (novel view synthesis) evaluated in scene-agnostic and per-scene finetuning settings.
- Representation rigidity: Coordinate-based 3D point inputs (x, y, z, d) with fixed evaluation image resolutions and defined source-view sampling.
- Model sharing vs specialization: One scene-agnostic model trained on multi-scene data with optional per-scene finetuning.
- Role of positional encoding: Fixed sine/cosine positional encodings applied to ray-cast point sequences.

---

### 14. Final Classification

**Single-task, single-domain.** The paper evaluates a single task, "novel view synthesis" (Abstract; II.A), using multi-view image datasets in the same modality ("multi-view datasets containing both synthetic data and real data" (IV.A)). It claims generalization to novel scenes within that modality (Fig. 2 caption; IV.D Results), with no evidence of multi-task or multi-modal evaluation.

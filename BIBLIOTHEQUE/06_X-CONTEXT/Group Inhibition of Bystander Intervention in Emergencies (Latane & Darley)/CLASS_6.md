# Group Inhibition of Bystander Intervention in Emergencies (1967)
Source: Group Inhibition of Bystander Intervention in Emergencies (Latane & Darley).md

## Core reasons
- The paper reports a social psychology experiment on bystander behavior in emergencies, not a machine learning method or dataset.
- It provides cross-disciplinary behavioral insights about social influence and intervention decisions.

## Evidence extracts
- "The subject, seated in a small waiting room, faced an ambiguous but potentially dangerous situation as a stream of smoke began to puff into the room through a wall vent." (Section METHOD)
- "Before an individual can decide to intervene in an emergency, he must, implicitly or explicitly, take several preliminary steps." (Section DISCUSSION)

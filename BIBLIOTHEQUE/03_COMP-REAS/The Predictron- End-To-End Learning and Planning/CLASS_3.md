# The Predictron: End-To-End Learning and Planning (2017)
Source: The Predictron- End-To-End Learning and Planning.md

## Core reasons
- The paper's central contribution is a new neural architecture for planning-like computation: an internal abstract model is unrolled for multiple imagined steps and accumulated into value predictions.
- It explicitly proposes computation changes (multi-step internal rollout, learned $\lambda$ accumulation, adaptive depth, and consistency updates), rather than positional encoding, dimensional adaptation, or benchmark creation.

## Evidence extracts
- "The predictron consists of a fully abstract model, represented by a Markov reward process, that can be rolled forward multiple \"imagined\" planning steps." (Abstract)
- "In this paper we introduce a new architecture, which we call the predictron, that integrates learning and planning into one end-to-end training procedure." (Section 1. Introduction)
- "This enables the predictron to compute to an adaptive depth (Graves, 2016) depending on the internal state and learning dynamics of the network." (Section 3. Predictron architecture)

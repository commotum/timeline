# Graph of Thoughts: Solving Elaborate Problems with Large Language Models (Not specified in the paper.)
Source: Graph of Thoughts (GoT).md

## Core reasons
- Proposes a new prompting framework that changes how reasoning is structured by modeling LLM thoughts as an arbitrary graph with dependencies.
- Emphasizes graph-enabled thought transformations (e.g., aggregation, refinement) to enhance reasoning beyond Chain-of-Thought and Tree of Thoughts.

## Evidence extracts
- "We introduce Graph of Thoughts (GoT): a framework that advances prompting capabilities in large language models (LLMs) beyond those offered by paradigms such as Chain-of-Thought or Tree of Thoughts (ToT)." (Abstract)
- "We observe that these (and many other) thought transformations can be naturally enabled when modeling the reasoning process of an LLM as a graph." (Section 1 Introduction)

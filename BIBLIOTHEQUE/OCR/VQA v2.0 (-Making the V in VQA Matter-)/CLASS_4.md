# Making the V in VQA Matter: Elevating the Role of Image Understanding in Visual Question Answering (Not specified in the paper.)
Source: VQA v2.0 (-Making the V in VQA Matter-).md

## Core reasons
- Introduces a balanced VQA dataset by collecting complementary images per question to reduce language priors, positioning the dataset as the central contribution.
- Benchmarks existing VQA models on the balanced dataset to provide a measurement lens for model bias and progress.

## Evidence extracts
- "Specifically, we balance the popular VQA dataset [3] by collecting complementary images such that every question in our balanced dataset is associated with not just a single image, but rather a pair of similar images that result in two different answers to the question." (Abstract)
- "We further benchmark a number of state-of-art VQA models on our balanced dataset." (Abstract)

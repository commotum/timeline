# Physical Principles for Scalable Neural Recording (2013)
Source: Physical Principles for Scalable Neural Recording.md

## Core reasons
- The paper analyzes physical limits and techniques for recording brain activity, which is neuroscience/physics context rather than ML model development.
- It focuses on modalities like optical, electrical, magnetic resonance, and molecular recording, indicating cross-disciplinary foundations.

## Evidence extracts
- "Simultaneously measuring the activities of all neurons in a mammalian brain at millisecond resolution is a challenge beyond the limits of existing techniques in neuroscience." (Section Abstract)
- "Recording techniques have also diversified: activity-dependent optical signals from neurons endowed with fluorescent indicators can be measured by photodetectors, and radio-frequency emissions from excited nuclear spins allow the construction of magnetic resonance images modulated by activity-dependent contrast mechanisms." (Section 1 Introduction)

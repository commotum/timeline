## Core reasons
- Introduces BEIT-3 as a general-purpose multimodal foundation model with a unified pretraining approach, focusing on model architecture and training methodology rather than positional encoding or dataset creation.
- Emphasizes masked "language" modeling across images, texts, and image-text pairs and the use of Multiway Transformers as the backbone, reflecting a methodological contribution in pretraining.

## Evidence extracts
- "In this work, we introduce a general-purpose multimodal foundation model **BEIT-3**, which achieves state-of-the-art transfer performance on both vision and vision-language tasks." (Abstract)
- "The key idea of BEIT-3 is that image can be modeled as a foreign language, so that we can conduct masked \"language\" modeling over images, texts, and image-text pairs in a unified way. We also demonstrate that Multiway Transformers can effectively model different vision and vision-language tasks, making it an intriguing option for general-purpose modeling." (4 Conclusion)

# Toy Models of Superposition (2022)
Source: Toy Models of Superposition.md

## Core reasons
- Studies how neural network representations exhibit polysemanticity and superposition using toy ReLU networks, aiming to explain interpretability phenomena rather than proposing a new architecture or encoding.
- Provides theoretical framing and empirical evidence (e.g., phase change, geometric structure) about representation properties, which is a foundational ML contribution.

## Evidence extracts
- "This paper provides a toy model where polysemanticity can be fully understood, arising as a result of models storing additional sparse features in \"superposition.\" We demonstrate the existence of a phase change, a surprising connection to the geometry of uniform polytopes, and evidence of a link to adversarial examples." (Abstract)
- "Roughly, the idea of superposition is that neural networks \"want to represent more features than they have neurons\", so they exploit a property of high-dimensional spaces to simulate a model with many more neurons." (The Superposition Hypothesis)

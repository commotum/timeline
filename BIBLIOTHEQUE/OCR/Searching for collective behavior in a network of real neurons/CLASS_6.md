## Core reasons
- The study analyzes biological neural recordings and collective spiking activity in the salamander retina, which is neuroscience rather than an ML model contribution.
- The work applies maximum entropy/statistical mechanics to explain neural population behavior, providing cross-disciplinary context rather than proposing ML architectures, datasets, or mechanisms.

## Evidence extracts
- "We have applied the maximum entropy framework to the analysis of one large experimental data set on the responses of ganglion cells in the salamander retina to a repeated, naturalistic movie." (Section III. CAN WE LEARN THE MODEL?)

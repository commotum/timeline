## Core reasons
- The paper centers on a Transformer that takes raw video, audio, and text signals as inputs, extending Transformer modeling to multimodal, higher-dimensional domains beyond 1D language.
- It adapts the Transformer pipeline with modality-specific tokenization and pre-training on video/audio/text rather than proposing a new positional encoding or a benchmark resource.

## Evidence extracts
- "Specifically, our Video-Audio-Text Transformer (VATT) takes raw signals as inputs and extracts multimodal representations that are rich enough to benefit a variety of downstream tasks." (Abstract)
- "To this end, we study self-supervised, multimodal pre-training of three Transformers [88], which take as input the raw RGB frames of internet videos, audio waveforms, and text transcripts of the speech audio, respectively." (1 Introduction)

# Bidirectional Recurrent Neural Networks (1997)
Source: Bidirectional Recurrent Neural Networks (Schuster & Paliwal).md

## Core reasons
- Proposes a new neural network architecture (BRNN) to use both past and future context, extending standard RNN structure and training.
- The contribution is an architectural modeling improvement with experiments, not a dataset/benchmark or positional encoding change.

## Evidence extracts
- "we propose a bidirectional recurrent neural network (BRNN) that can be trained using all available input information in the past and future of a specific time frame" (Section II.B. Bidirectional Recurrent Neural Networks)
- "a simple extension to a regular recurrent neural network structure has been presented, which makes it possible to train the network in both time directions simultaneously." (Section IV. Discussion and Conclusion)

# Vokenization: Improving Language Understanding with Contextualized, Visual-Grounded Supervision (Not specified in the paper.)
Source: Vokenization- Improving Language Understanding with Contextualized, Visual-Grounded Supervision.md

## Core reasons
- Proposes a new training method for language models: vokenization that maps tokens to images to provide visual supervision during pre-training.
- Introduces a voken-classification objective added to standard language pre-training (e.g., BERT) rather than modifying positional encoding or model dimensionality.

## Evidence extracts
- "Therefore, we develop a technique named \"vokenization\" that extrapolates multimodal alignments to language-only data by contextually mapping language tokens to their related images (which we call \"vokens\")." (Abstract)
- "Based on these vokens, we propose a new pre-training task for language: voken classification." (Section 2.1 Vokens: Visualized Tokens)

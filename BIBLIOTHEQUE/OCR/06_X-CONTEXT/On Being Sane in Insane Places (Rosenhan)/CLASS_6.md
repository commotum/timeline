# On Being Sane in Insane Places (1973)
Source: On Being Sane in Insane Places (Rosenhan).md

## Core reasons
- The paper is a psychology/psychiatry experiment about diagnosing sanity and insanity in psychiatric hospitals, not a machine learning contribution.
- Its focus is on diagnostic labeling, depersonalization, and hospital staff behavior, providing cross-disciplinary context rather than ML methods or datasets.

## Evidence extracts
- "The eight pseudopatients were a varied group. One was a psychology graduate student in his 20s. The remaining seven were older and \"established.\" Among them were three psychologists, a pediatrician, a psychiatrist, a painter, and a housewife." (Section "PSEUDOPATIENTS AND THEIR SETTINGS")
- "Despite their public \"show\" of sanity, the pseudopatients were never detected. Admitted, except in one case, with a diagnosis of schizophrenia, 10 each was discharged with a diagnosis of schizophrenia \"in remission.\"" (Section "THE NORMAL ARE NOT DETECTABLY SANE")

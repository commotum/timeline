# Thermodynamics for a network of neurons: Signatures of criticality (2014)
Source: Thermodynamics for a network of neurons- Signatures of criticality.md

## Core reasons
- The paper is a neuroscience/physics study using statistical mechanics to analyze neural population activity, not a machine learning model or method.
- Its contribution is cross-disciplinary theoretical framing of neural data in thermodynamic/criticality terms rather than ML architecture, benchmarks, or optimization.

## Evidence extracts
- "The activity of a neural network is defined by patterns of spiking and silence from the individual neurons." (Section I. INTRODUCTION)
- "Statistical mechanics provides a language for connecting the interactions among many microscopic degrees of freedom to the macroscopic behavior of matter." (Section I. INTRODUCTION)

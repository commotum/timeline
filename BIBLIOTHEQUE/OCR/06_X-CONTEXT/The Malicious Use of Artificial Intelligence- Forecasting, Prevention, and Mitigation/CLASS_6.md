## Core reasons
- This is a security and policy report surveying malicious uses of AI and proposing prevention/mitigation approaches, not a model or algorithm contribution.
- It frames AI risks across security domains and emphasizes institutional responses, placing it outside core ML method development.

## Evidence extracts

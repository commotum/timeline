# In-context Learning and Induction Heads (2022)
Source: In-context Learning and Induction Heads.md

## Core reasons
- The paper is a mechanistic interpretability study that aims to explain in-context learning in transformer models, which fits ML foundations and principles rather than proposing new architectures or datasets.
- It presents evidence about induction heads as a mechanistic explanation for in-context learning, not a new positional encoding, dimensional lifting, or benchmark.

## Evidence extracts
- "we present preliminary and indirect evidence for a hypothesis that induction heads might constitute the mechanism for the majority of all \"incontext learning\" in large transformer models" (Abstract)
- "We present six complementary lines of evidence, arguing that induction heads may be the mechanistic source of general in-context learning in transformer models of any size." (Abstract)

## Core reasons
- Introduces BIG-bench as a benchmark resource for evaluating model capabilities across many tasks.
- Emphasizes measurement and evaluation (including human baselines) rather than proposing a new model architecture.

## Evidence extracts
- "To address this challenge, we introduce the Beyond the Imitation Game benchmark (BIGbench)." (Abstract)
- "Motivated by the importance of predicting the potentially transformative effects of large language models, and by the limitations of current benchmarks, we introduce a large-scale, extremely difficult and diverse benchmark. We then measure model performance on this benchmark. We provide a human-evaluator baseline and expert human evaluations on this diverse set of tasks, in order to measure whether model performance is broadly distinguishable from human-evaluator performance." (Section 1.3 Beyond the imitation game)

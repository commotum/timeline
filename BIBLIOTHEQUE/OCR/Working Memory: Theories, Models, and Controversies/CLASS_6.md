# Working Memory: Theories, Models, and Controversies (2012)
Source: Working Memory: Theories, Models, and Controversies.md

## Core reasons
- The paper is a psychology review of working memory theories and models, not a machine learning contribution.
- It provides cognitive science foundations (phonological loop, central executive, episodic buffer) that serve as external context for ML rather than ML methods.

## Evidence extracts
- "I present an account of the origins and development of the multicomponent approach to working memory, making a distinction between the overall theoretical framework, which has remained relatively stable, and the attempts to build more specific models within this framework." (Abstract)
- "The basis is the assumption that it is useful to postulate a hypothetical limitedcapacity system that provides the temporary storage and manipulation of information that is necessary for performing a wide range of cognitive activities." (Evolution of a Multicomponent Theory)

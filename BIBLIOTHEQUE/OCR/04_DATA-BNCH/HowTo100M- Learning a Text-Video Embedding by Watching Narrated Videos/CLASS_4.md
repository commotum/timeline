# HowTo100M: Learning a Text-Video Embedding by Watching Hundred Million Narrated Video Clips (2019)
Source: HowTo100M- Learning a Text-Video Embedding by Watching Narrated Videos.md

## Core reasons
- Introduces the HowTo100M dataset as the main contribution, emphasizing its large scale and automatic narration-based captions.
- Frames the work around releasing this dataset/resource and demonstrating its utility for text-video embedding benchmarks.

## Evidence extracts
- "First, we introduce HowTo100M: a large-scale dataset of 136 million video clips sourced from 1.22M narrated instructional web videos depicting humans performing and describing over 23k different visual tasks." (Abstract)
- "We have introduced HowTo100M, a video dataset with more than 130M video clips, extracted from 1.2M narrated web videos of people performing complex visual tasks." (Conclusion)

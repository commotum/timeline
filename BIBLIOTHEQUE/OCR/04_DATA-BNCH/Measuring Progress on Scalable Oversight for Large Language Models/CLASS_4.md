## Core reasons
- The paper's main contribution is an experimental design (the sandwiching paradigm) for empirically evaluating scalable oversight rather than a new model architecture.
- It validates that design with existing benchmarks (MMLU and time-limited QuALITY) to measure oversight progress, emphasizing evaluation methodology and measurement.

## Evidence extracts
- "We first present an experimental design centered on tasks for which human specialists succeed but unaided humans and current general AI systems fail." (Abstract)
- "The Experiment: We show that two existing NLP tasks satisfy the constraints of sandwiching well with large language models and that a simple baseline strategy for language-model conversational agents—asking humans to elicit knowledge from them through conversation—works imperfectly but surprisingly well at producing high-quality labels on two hard question-answering tasks."

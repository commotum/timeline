## Core reasons
- Introduces a benchmark/diagnostic suite focused on evaluating LVLM failures like hallucination and visual illusion, which is a measurement resource.
- Provides a curated dataset of visual-question control pairs and describes its composition, indicating the primary contribution is the benchmark data.

## Evidence extracts
- "We present HALLUSIONBENCH, the first benchmark designed to examine visual illusion and knowledge hallucination of LVLMs and analyze the potential failure modes based on each hand-crafted example pair." (Section 3. HALLUSIONBENCH Construction)
- "HALLUSIONBENCH consists of 455 visual-question control pairs, including 346 different figures and a total of 1129 questions on diverse topics (including *food, math, geometry, statistics, geography, sports, cartoon, famous illusions, movie, meme, etc.*) and formats (including *logo, poster, figure, charts, table, map, consecutive images, etc.*)."

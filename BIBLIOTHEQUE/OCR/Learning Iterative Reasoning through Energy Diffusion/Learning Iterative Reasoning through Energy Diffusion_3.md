# Learning Iterative Reasoning through Energy Diffusion (2024)
Source: Learning Iterative Reasoning through Energy Diffusion.md

## Core reasons
- Proposes IRED, a framework that formulates reasoning and decision-making as energy-based optimization.
- Introduces adaptive, iterative optimization at inference time via energy functions to handle problem difficulty.

## Evidence extracts
- "We introduce iterative reasoning through energy diffusion (IRED), a novel framework for learning to reason for a variety of tasks by formulating reasoning and decision-making problems with energy-based optimization." (Abstract)
- "During inference, because we are explicitly solving an optimization problem of finding the  $y^*$  that maximizes the energy function  $E_\theta$ , we can run an adaptive number of optimization steps depending on the hardness of the problem." (1. Introduction)

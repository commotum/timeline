# Scaling Up Visual and Vision-Language Representation Learning With Noisy Text Supervision (2021)
Source: Scaling Up Vision-Language Learning With Noisy Text Supervision (ALIGN).md

## Core reasons
- Presents a representation-learning method that aligns image and text encoders with a contrastive loss, focusing on training methodology rather than positional encoding or dimensional lifting.
- Emphasizes scaling vision-language representation learning with large noisy image-text data to improve transfer and retrieval performance.

## Evidence extracts
- "In this paper, we leverage a noisy dataset of over one billion image alt-text pairs, obtained without expensive filtering or post-processing steps in the Conceptual Captions dataset. A simple dual-encoder architecture learns to align visual and language representations of the image and text pairs using a contrastive loss." (Abstract)
- "In this work, we leverage a dataset of over one billion noisy image alt-text pairs to scale visual and vision-language representation learning." (Section 1. Introduction)

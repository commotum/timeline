## Core reasons
- The paper introduces and studies a weak-to-strong learning setup for aligning strong models with weak supervision, which is a training/learning methodology contribution rather than a new architecture or positional encoding.
- The main contributions are methods to improve generalization under weak supervision (auxiliary losses, bootstrapping, unsupervised finetuning), fitting ML foundations and principles.

## Evidence extracts
- "We find that when we naively finetune strong pretrained models on labels generated by a weak model, they consistently perform better than their weak supervisors, a phenomenon we call weak-to-strong generalization." (Abstract)
- "We find that we can improve performance by encouraging strong models to have confident predictions with an auxiliary loss, bootstrapping supervision with intermediate models, and improving model representations with unsupervised finetuning." (Section 1 Introduction)

## Core reasons
- Studies counter-intuitive properties of deep neural networks, focusing on interpretability of representations and robustness, rather than proposing new architectures, datasets, or positional encodings.
- Provides analysis and evidence about semantic meaning in representation spaces and the existence of adversarial examples, which fits foundational principles about model behavior.

## Evidence extracts
- "First, we find that there is no distinction between individual high level units and random linear combinations of high level units, according to various methods of unit analysis." (Abstract)
- "Specifically, we show that by using a simple optimization procedure, we are able to find adversarial examples, which are obtained by imperceptibly small perturbations to a correctly classified input image, so that it is no longer classified correctly." (Section 4 Blind Spots in Neural Networks)

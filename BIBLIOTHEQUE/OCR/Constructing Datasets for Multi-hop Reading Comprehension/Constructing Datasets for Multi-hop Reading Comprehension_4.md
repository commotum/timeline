## Core reasons
- The paper proposes a new cross-document multi-hop reading comprehension task and centers on how to construct datasets for it.
- The main contribution is a dataset induction methodology and the creation of two datasets (WIKIHOP and MEDHOP), with evaluation as supporting work.

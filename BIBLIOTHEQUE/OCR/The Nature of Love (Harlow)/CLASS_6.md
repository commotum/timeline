# The Nature of Love (1958)
Source: The Nature of Love (Harlow).md

## Core reasons
- The paper is a psychology address reporting primate experiments on affectional responses, not an ML method or dataset.
- It provides cross-disciplinary context about attachment and contact comfort that could inform ML, but it is not an ML contribution.

## Evidence extracts
- "Many of these difficulties can be resolved by the use of the neonatal and infant macaque monkey as the subject for the analysis of basic affectional variables." (Section "The Nature of Love")
- "At this point we decided to study the development of affectional responses of neonatal and infant monkeys to an artificial, inanimate mother, and so we built a surrogate mother which we hoped and believed would be a good surrogate mother." (Section "The Nature of Love")

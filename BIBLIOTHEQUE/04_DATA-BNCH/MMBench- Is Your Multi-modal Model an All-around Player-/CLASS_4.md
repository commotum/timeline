# MMBench: Is Your Multi-modal Model an All-around Player? (2024)
Source: MMBench- Is Your Multi-modal Model an All-around Player-.md

## Core reasons
- The paper's main contribution is a benchmark dataset for evaluating multimodal models, emphasizing objective measurement and coverage of abilities.
- It introduces an evaluation pipeline/strategy (CircularEval and choice extraction) as part of the benchmark infrastructure rather than a new model architecture.

## Evidence extracts
- "we propose MMBench, a bilingual benchmark for assessing the multi-modal capabilities of VLMs." (Abstract)
- "Currently, MMBench contains over 3000 multiple-choice questions covering 20 different ability dimensions, such as object localization and social reasoning, for evaluating vision-language models." (1 Introduction)
